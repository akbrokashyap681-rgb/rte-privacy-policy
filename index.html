Privacy Policy — RTE Help Tutorial

Effective Date: November 16, 2026

Disclaimer (Non-Government App): RTE Help Tutorial is an independent, unofficial guidance and tutorial app created based on practical experience. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an official app of the Government of Rajasthan or the RTE / PSP Portal. Official portal: rajpsp.rajasthan.gov.in.

1. Information Collection and Use

RTE Help Tutorial does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, login credentials, Aadhaar numbers, NIC IDs, phone numbers, or student records on any external server.

2. Local On-Device Processing & Storage

Features such as the Date of Birth eligibility checker, language selection, and dismissed announcements run locally on your device. Any date or preference you select is stored only on your device and is never uploaded as personal data.

3. Live Content Updates & Embedded Documents

To keep tutorial guides and announcements up to date, the app fetches non-personal, read-only public guide updates via Google Firebase Firestore and displays official sample forms/PDFs hosted on Google Drive. These requests do not collect or transmit any personal user data.

4. Third-Party Links & Sharing

The app contains links to the official Rajasthan Private School / RTE Portal (rajpsp.rajasthan.gov.in) and optional sharing links (such as WhatsApp). When you open external links, they open in your device's browser or respective app and are governed by their own privacy policies.

5. Advertisements & Analytics

This app does not display third-party advertisements and does not use tracking or behavioral analytics SDKs.

6. Children's Privacy

Since no personal data is collected from any user, the app is safe for parents, teachers, and school staff.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you may contact the developer at akbrokashyap681@gmail.com or via the developer contact details listed on the app store page (PhonePe Appstore / Google Play Store).
